## Rename Step Flake

In run 36983457984, `PopulatedWorkflowTests.testEarlyPhotoDevelopmentDarkroomAndRemoval` renamed the Film to "Renamed synthetic FilmFilm".
The test tapped the center of the Rename alert's title field and then deleted one character per title character.
A tap on a field that already has focus moves the cursor to the nearest word boundary, so the deletes left "Film" behind.
This was reproduced outside the pipeline on an export of `145e11a`, on an iOS 26.5 iPhone 17 Pro simulator pinned to CI's light appearance and large text.
With the field focused, the center tap left exactly "Film" in 2 of 2 runs, and a tap past the end of the title cleared the field in 2 of 2.
`2bf73b7` taps past the end of the title and asserts the field is empty before typing; the final-title assertion is unchanged.
The full test with that change passed 5 of 5 locally and 12 of 12 harness tests passed in run 36995398670.
It is a test-robustness fix, not an app change.
