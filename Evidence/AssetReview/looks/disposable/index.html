The Format Reference names and every reference image stay internal and never ship in the app. Reference images are public-domain or openly licensed photographs credited under each one; many are scans of surviving prints or reels, which have aged, so judge them for the format's character as it was new. The test scenes are modern digital photographs, not captures from an iPhone running the app, so capture behavior (fixed exposure, the real flash) is not shown.

Format Reference
Kodak Fun Saver 35mm single-use camera with flash, loaded with 800-speed color film
PRD 2.1 section 6
Borderless 3:2 picture with no date stamp. Warm, saturated color, heavy film grain, harsh direct flash with flat shadows, and soft edges. No light leaks, red-eye, or dust and scratches. Fixed exposure: a dim scene without flash develops dark and grainy, and flash lights only nearby subjects.
What to judge
  • Color: warm and saturated like a 1990s minilab print, never yellowed or faded.
  • Grain: heavy, as 800-speed film shows it; heavier in the dim, unflashed scene.
  • Flash: harsh, direct, flat on the subject, with the background falling away.
  • Lens: soft, slightly darker corners from the plastic lens.
  • Nothing the format did not make: no light leaks, red-eye, dust or scratches.

Values in the app

From FilmLook.swift: the one place each Camera's and Film Stock's look is set. Spatial values are fractions of the picture's long edge.

    /// 1990s Disposable. Format Reference: the Kodak Fun Saver single-use camera with flash and 800-speed color
    /// negative film, printed by a minilab. Warm, saturated color with heavy grain that grows in an underexposed dim
    /// scene, a plastic lens's soft, darker corners, and harsh direct flash that lights the subject flat and lets the
    /// background fall away. No light leaks, red-eye, dust or scratches (PRD 2.1 section 6.1).
    static let disposable = Treatment(
        emulsion: .color(ColorEmulsion(
            warmth: 0.055, layers: Layers(greenFromBlue: -0.05), saturation: 1.1,
            tone: ToneCurve(contrast: 1.1, midtones: 0.88, black: 0.015, white: 0.99),
            shadows: RGB(-0.004, 0.008, 0.004), highlights: RGB(0.004, 0.003, -0.006),
            grain: Grain(amplitude: 8, size: 0.0009, chroma: 0.3, shadows: 0.6)
        )),
        lens: Lens(edgeSoftness: 0.0012, edgeDarkening: 0.6),
        flash: Flash(falloff: 0.9, contrast: 0.1)
    )
